BOOK-BASED DESIGN
  - Keep the three-area R1/R2 backbone design with R3 in Area 1 and R4 in Area 2.
  - First advertise external static routes from R4 and observe Type-5 LSAs.
  - Convert Area 1 to totally stubby by configuring stub on both ends and
    no-summaries at the ABR.
  - Convert Area 1 to NSSA, originate local external routes inside it, and verify
    Type-7 LSAs and Type-7-to-Type-5 translation by the ABR.
  - The book uses an additional R5 running RIP to feed R3. This lab adapts that
    part to static discard routes on R3 to preserve the four-cRPD physical design.
  - A standard Stub stage is included as an extra intermediate learning step.
  - The optional NSSA no-summaries stage reflects the chapter's final note and
    current Juniper syntax guidance.

NOTE ABOUT DEFAULT-LSA TYPE
  The uploaded ebook's standard NSSA example includes a default-lsa type-7 line
  while summaries are enabled. Current Juniper documentation describes the
  type-7 option specifically for the no-summaries NSSA case. Therefore, this
  lab's normal NSSA stage uses default-lsa default-metric with summaries enabled;
  the explicit type-7 default option is introduced in the NSSA no-summaries stage.
  The Type-7 LSAs for 192.168.71.0/24 and 192.168.72.0/24 are independently
  originated by R3 throughout the NSSA stages.
