Conventions
- The VLAN number equals the two-digit router pair, e.g. R1-R2 = VLAN 12 and Junos unit eth1.12.
- Point-to-point IPv4 links use /30; the lower-numbered router uses .1 and the higher-numbered router uses .2.
- Baseline OSPF costs are the default 1. Do not add explicit interface metrics to baseline files.
- The lab reuses the parent interfaces connected to the externally managed `br-univ`; do not manipulate the host bridge.
