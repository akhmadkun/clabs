Objective
1.   Become comfortable with Junos operational and configuration modes.
2.   Verify physical and logical interface state.
3.   Understand VLAN membership, access ports and a basic trunk.
4.   Verify MAC learning and endpoint connectivity.
5.   Practice safe Junos configuration workflow with compare/commit/rollback.
Tasks
–   Access both SW2 and SW3 and identify hostname, model and Junos version.
–   Check interface state.
–   Confige VLAN Student and Teacher, access and trunk port.
–   Demonstrate failed commit for non-existent vlans configuration.
–   Demonstrate show | compare and rollback.
–   Demonstrate adding more than 1 vlans on access ports.
–   Verify VLAN configuration
–   Verify the host-facing port is an access port carrying a single VLAN.
–   Verify the inter-switch link is a trunk carrying VLAN Student and Teacher.
–   Verify MAC learning on both switches.
–   Test end-to-end ping on each VLANs.
–   Demonstrate removing Teacher VLAN on the trunk port.
–   Demonstrate rollback to the previous state.
