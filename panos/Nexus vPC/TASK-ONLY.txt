LAB-001 — NX-OS Titanium ↔ IOL LACP / EtherChannel

Objective
Build a two-link LACP Port-Channel between Cisco NX-OS Titanium 7.3.0.D1.1 and Cisco IOL 17.15.01, then verify forwarding and member-link failure behavior.

Topology

             LACP Po10
        ====================
        NX1                IOL1
    Eth2/1 ------------- Eth0/1
    Eth2/2 ------------- Eth0/2

Port-Channel addressing
NX1   Po10: 10.10.10.1/30
IOL1  Po10: 10.10.10.2/30

Loopbacks
NX1   Lo0: 10.255.0.1/32
IOL1  Lo0: 10.255.0.2/32

Tasks

1. Deploy the lab.

2. On NX1, verify:
   - both physical interfaces are up
   - Po10 is up
   - both members are bundled
   - LACP neighbor is IOL1

3. On IOL1, verify:
   - Port-channel10 is up
   - both members are bundled
   - LACP neighbor is NX1

4. Test traffic:
   - ping 10.10.10.2 from NX1
   - ping 10.10.10.1 from IOL1
   - ping 10.255.0.2 source loopback0 from NX1
   - ping 10.255.0.1 source loopback0 from IOL1

5. Failure test:
   - keep continuous ping running across Po10
   - shut down NX1 Ethernet2/1
   - verify Po10 stays up with one member
   - verify traffic continues
   - restore Ethernet2/1
   - shut down NX1 Ethernet2/2
   - verify Po10 stays up with the remaining member
   - restore Ethernet2/2

6. Final verification:
   - confirm both members return to bundled/up state
   - confirm loopback-to-loopback traffic works again

Expected outcome
Po10 remains operational when either single LACP member is lost. Traffic should continue over the remaining member.
