LAB-019 — OSPF NODE-LINK PROTECTION

• Establish the OSPF baseline on the existing R1–R4 core topology:
  ◦ Use OSPFv2 in Area 0 with point-to-point links.
  ◦ R1 is vJunos-router and acts as the point of local repair (PLR).
  ◦ R2, R3 and R4 are the existing cRPD peer nodes.
  ◦ Reuse VLAN 12 (R1–R2), VLAN 13 (R1–R3), VLAN 24 (R2–R4) and VLAN 34 (R3–R4).
  ◦ Use R4 loopback 4.4.4.4/32 as the protected destination.
  ◦ Verify all four OSPF adjacencies are Full.
  ◦ Verify R1's primary route to 4.4.4.4/32 is via 10.0.12.2 / ge-0/0/0.12, metric 2.
  ◦ Verify R1's alternate neighbor is R3 via 10.0.13.3 / ge-0/0/0.13.
  ◦ Preserve the observed R1 outgoing OSPF cost of 10 on ge-0/0/0.13.

• Configure OSPF node-link protection on R1 as follows:
  ◦ Protect the primary interface ge-0/0/0.12 toward R2.
  ◦ Replace link-protection with node-link-protection; do not leave both statements on the interface.
  ◦ Keep all existing IP addresses, router IDs, VLAN IDs, OSPF areas and interface types unchanged.

• Verify node-protecting LFA calculation for destination 4.4.4.4/32:
  ◦ Confirm R3 is an eligible backup next-hop for the destination.
  ◦ Verify R3 reaches 4.4.4.4/32 without traversing R2.
  ◦ Collect the live route metrics from R3 and R2.
  ◦ Prove the RFC 5286 node-protecting condition:
      D(R3,R4) < D(R3,R2) + D(R2,R4)
  ◦ Use live route metrics; do not substitute an interface cost or the R1 backup-SPF "Self to Neighbor Metric" for these distances.

• Prepare and verify forwarding-table protection:
  ◦ Inspect the existing routing-options forwarding-table export and policy-options configuration.
  ◦ Ensure a per-packet load-balancing policy is applied for OSPF backup next-hop installation.
  ◦ Preserve any existing forwarding-table export policy; if no export policy exists, use a lab-scoped policy.
  ◦ Record the RIB, Routing Engine forwarding-table and PFE route/next-hop outputs for 4.4.4.4/32.

• Perform a controlled R2 node-failure test:
  ◦ Start a sustained rapid ping from R1 to 4.4.4.4/32.
  ◦ From the Containerlab host, stop only node R2 using the deployed topology file or lab name.
  ◦ Do not stop R1, R3 or R4; do not destroy or redeploy the topology.
  ◦ Verify R1–R2 and R2–R4 become unavailable while R1–R3 and R3–R4 remain operational.
  ◦ Record packet loss, sequence gaps, RTT changes, timestamps, route changes and OSPF neighbor states.
  ◦ Verify R1 ultimately reaches 4.4.4.4/32 through R3, avoiding R2.

• Restore the topology and the intended protection state:
  ◦ Start only node R2 and wait for all four OSPF adjacencies to return to Full.
  ◦ Verify R1's primary route to 4.4.4.4/32 returns through R2 at metric 2.
  ◦ Confirm node-link backup coverage is recalculated.
  ◦ Restore LAB-018 link-protection on R1 if returning to the LAB-018 state.
  ◦ Remove only the lab-scoped policy created for LAB-019, if one was added and no such policy existed beforehand.

• Once complete, R1 must have a verified node-protecting alternate through R3 for 4.4.4.4/32, the R2 node-failure behavior must be documented, and the original topology must be fully restored.

IMPORTANT
  A route switch through R3 after global OSPF convergence proves reachability recovery, not by itself local repair. A 0% ping-loss result alone is not sufficient to claim PFE fast reroute. Report LFA calculation, forwarding preparation, traffic behavior and actual local-repair evidence separately. Do not claim a sub-50-ms result from this lab unless independently measured and supported by suitable evidence.
