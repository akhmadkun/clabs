CLEANUP BEFORE LAB-011

IMPORTANT
- Stop other OSPF labs before using this address/VLAN space.
- Do not modify, destroy, or recreate `br-univ`.
- These commands remove the earlier LAB-010 OSPFv3 control-plane configuration and the relevant eth1 logical units from the routers participating in or adjacent to LAB-010. Do this only when you have finished with the previous lab and are ready to switch contexts.
- Do not remove `lo0.0` or the base startup-config.

On each listed router, enter `configure`, apply its cleanup lines, then `commit check` and `commit`.

  delete protocols ospf3

  delete interfaces eth1 unit 14
