JUNIPER EX SERIES OPERATIONS & TROUBLESHOOTING
LAB-001 — Junos CLI, Interfaces, VLAN & Access Ports

Objective
1. Become comfortable with Junos operational and configuration modes.
2. Verify physical and logical interface state.
3. Understand VLAN membership, access ports and a basic trunk.
4. Verify MAC learning and endpoint connectivity.
5. Practice safe Junos configuration workflow with compare/commit/rollback.

Tasks
1. Access both switches and identify hostname, model and Junos version.
2. Check interface state with `show interfaces terse`.
3. Inspect VLAN and switching configuration.
4. Verify the host-facing port is an access port in VLAN USERS.
5. Verify the inter-switch link is a trunk carrying VLAN USERS.
6. Verify MAC learning on both switches.
7. Ping HOST2 from HOST1.
8. Make one harmless configuration change, inspect `show | compare`, then commit.
9. Demonstrate rollback to the previous state.
10. Re-verify VLAN, MAC learning and end-host connectivity.

Rule: do not open the solution guide on the first attempt.
